01 // About

From telecom operations to enterprise program leadership.

I started my career in 2009 as a Team Lead at NTT DATA, managing tariff operations and digital front-end initiatives for T-Mobile and Orange — and building automated reporting solutions that turned manual monitoring into informed decision-making.

That operational grounding grew into global delivery leadership at Capgemini — running PMO operations across India, UK, Netherlands and UAE, delivering the multi-million-euro Grolsch SAP implementation with zero-error audits — followed by an automation-first chapter at Gradmener, implementing Robotic Process Automation that redesigned manual workflows into structured, scalable processes.

Today I work at the intersection of program management, portfolio governance and financial oversight — currently as a Senior Program Management Consultant at Capco, leading strategic banking transformation for HSBC WPB Asset Management with charters, roadmaps, governance models, Power BI portfolio intelligence and AI-enabled automation that keeps execution predictable. Outside enterprise delivery, I build AI products hands-on — PM Control Tower, Jigglypuff and The Fix — designed end-to-end with AI-assisted development and shipped as live, working prototypes.

APP // 02Voice AI

Jigglypuff
